### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

##### What should I do immediately after water damage occurs?

Stop the water source if safe and avoid entering affected areas with standing water. Call us immediately to extract water, dry structures, and prevent mold or structural damage.

##### How quickly does mold grow after water damage?

Mold can develop within 24–48 hours after water intrusion. Prompt drying and professional remediation are essential to prevent mold growth and protect your property and health.

##### Does Overland Restoration 24/7 work with insurance claims in Cape Coral & the surrounding Southwest Florida areas?

Yes, we assist with insurance claims, documenting damage and providing detailed reports. We help ensure proper restoration and smooth claim processing without shortcuts.

##### Why choose an owner-operated restoration company?

Working with us means direct communication with the owner throughout the process. This ensures accountability, personal attention, and higher-quality restoration compared to large rotating crews.

##### Can water-damaged electronics be salvaged?

Yes, we assess electronics on-site and advise on drying or repair techniques. Quick action improves the likelihood of recovery and reduces permanent damage.

##### Is mold remediation safe for children and pets?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard containment, air filtration, and non-toxic cleaning methods to ensure the safety of your family and pets during remediation.

##### How long does the restoration process take?

Restoration timelines depend on the damage type and severity. We provide accurate estimates after inspection and keep you informed throughout the process for efficient, timely recovery.

##### Do you provide preventative advice after restoration?

Yes, we educate clients on moisture control, ventilation, and maintenance strategies to prevent future water or mold issues, keeping homes and businesses safe.
